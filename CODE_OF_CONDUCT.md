# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Natural a harassment-free experience for everyone. Be respectful,
assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and prioritize a safe community. Maintainers
may remove contributions or participants whose behavior is abusive, discriminatory, threatening, or
persistently disruptive. Report conduct concerns privately to conduct@naturalengine.dev.

This policy is based on Contributor Covenant 2.1.
